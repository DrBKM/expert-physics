Shipping and delivery policy

How and when you receive study material, mock tests, classes and printed books.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Digital purchases

  1. Study material and mock tests are digital. Nothing is shipped and there are no shipping charges.
  2. Access is unlocked automatically on the website immediately after successful payment (usually within a few seconds; at most a few minutes). You can then view the material online or, for “View + Download” and “Full Access”, download the PDF. Mock tests appear with a Start test button.
  3. Sign in with the same account you used to buy, and press Refresh if needed.
  4. If your purchase is not unlocked within 1 hour of payment, contact us with your registered email and Razorpay Payment ID; we will resolve it within 48 hours or refund you as per our Refund & cancellation policy.

Online classes

The Google Meet / Zoom link and timetable are sent by email or WhatsApp after enrolment, before the first class.

Printed books

  1. Books are sent to the delivery address given in the book order form, within India.
  2. The dispatch date, the courier and any delivery charge are confirmed to you by WhatsApp or email when the order is accepted, before the balance is paid.
  3. Tracking details are shared once the book is dispatched.
