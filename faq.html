Frequently asked questions

Classes, notes, mock tests, payments and your account.

Classes

How are the classes held?
Live and online, on Google Meet or Zoom, in small batches. After you enrol, the class link and the timetable reach you by email or WhatsApp before the first class.
Can I attend a class before paying?
Yes. The first 30-minute class is a free trial. Fill in the enquiry form to book it.
How do I pay class fees?
By Google Pay, PhonePe, bank transfer or cash. The amounts are in the fee structure.

Study material

What is the difference between View and View + Download?
View lets you read a chapter in the browser, marked with your email address. View + Download also gives you the PDF. If you bought View, you can upgrade later by paying only the difference.
What does Full Access include?
Every chapter with view and download, every paid mock test, and all material added later, for a single one-time payment.
Can I share the notes or the PDF?
No. Your purchase is for your own study only. Sharing, uploading or reselling the material ends your access without a refund. See the terms.

Mock tests

How long is a test?
One minute per question. The timer starts when you press Start test and keeps running if you switch tabs. The test submits itself when time is up.
What happens if my internet drops during a test?
Your answers are saved on your device. Open the test again on the same device while time remains and you continue where you stopped.
How many times can I take a test?
Free tests: as often as you like. Paid tests: up to three attempts. Your best score is shown in the list.
Is there negative marking?
Only where the test says so. The marks for a right and a wrong answer are shown before you start each test.
How do I get a free test?
Finish four different tests in one category and you can unlock one paid test in that category for free. Your progress is shown above the test list.

Payments and refunds

How can I pay online?
Through Razorpay, by UPI (Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm), debit or credit card, net banking or wallet. The price is fixed on the payment page.
I paid but the item is still locked. What now?
Come back to the site and press Refresh. Payments are checked again automatically every few minutes, even if you closed the page. If it is still locked after an hour, send your registered email and the Razorpay Payment ID (it starts with “pay_”) on WhatsApp or email.
Where do I use a coupon code?
Type it into the coupon box on the payment panel before you pay. The new price is shown before you pay.
Can I get a refund?
Digital purchases are non-refundable once unlocked, except for failed, duplicate or never-unlocked payments. Class fees are refundable before the batch starts. Details are in the refund and cancellation policy.

Your account

How do I sign in?
With your mobile number and a one-time code by SMS, or with your email and password. Use the same account every time so your purchases and scores stay together.
I forgot my password.
On the sign-in panel, choose Forgot password. A 6-digit code goes to your email (and to Telegram if you linked it).
Can I get payment receipts on Telegram?
Yes, optionally. Link Telegram once from the payment panel and every receipt reaches you there.
