Enrolment and fees

Four steps from your first message to your first class. The trial class is free.

How to enrol

  1. Send an enquiryFill in the form below or message on WhatsApp with your class, board or university and target exam.
  2. Take the free trial classA 30-minute online class, so you can see the teaching style before you pay anything.
  3. Choose a batch and payPay the fee by Google Pay, PhonePe, bank transfer or cash.
  4. Get your class linkThe Google Meet or Zoom link and the timetable reach you by email or WhatsApp before the first class.

Fees

Class fees depend on the programme and the batch. The full list is in the fee structure document.

Open the fee structure

Study material and mock tests are bought separately in the student portal and paid online through Razorpay. Prices are shown next to each item.

Cancelling before the first class of a batch gets a full refund. See the refund and cancellation policy.
