Career paths in physics

Where physics can take you, step by step, and which exam opens each door.

  1. After Class X

    Choose science with physics, chemistry and mathematics or biology. Physics is common to NEET, JEE and every science degree.

  2. After Class XII

    NEET for medicine, JEE Main and Advanced for engineering, or a B.Sc. in physics through CUET-UG or your state university’s admission process.

  3. After B.Sc.

    M.Sc. through IIT-JAM, CUET-PG or university entrance tests, or an integrated Ph.D. through JEST. Government jobs and school teaching (TET and school service exams) also open up here.

  4. After M.Sc.

    CSIR-NET for a junior research fellowship or lectureship, GATE for research, M.Tech and public-sector jobs, and JEST or TIFR GS for Ph.D. places at research institutes.

  5. Research and teaching

    A Ph.D., then postdoctoral research in India or abroad, or a college teaching post after NET or SET.

Government jobs with any degree

WBCS and other state PSC exams, SSC CGL and CHSL, and RRB exams all test general science, where physics carries a good share of the marks.

The job exams programme and the job exam mock tests cover these.
