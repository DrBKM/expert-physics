Privacy policy

What information we collect, why, who it is shared with, and your choices.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

This policy explains how Expert Physics, operated by Bipul Kumar Mahato, Pratyusha, #03-A, Purulia 723101, handles your personal information.

1. Information we collect

  • Account details: your name, email address and, if you use mobile sign-in, your mobile number. Passwords are stored only as a salted one-way hash, never as text.
  • Purchases: what you bought, the amount, any coupon used and the Razorpay payment reference. Card, UPI and bank details are handled by Razorpay and never reach us.
  • Mock tests: your attempts, answers, scores and time taken.
  • Reviews: the rating and text you choose to publish. Only your first name and the initial of your surname are shown publicly.
  • Optional details: your Telegram chat ID if you link Telegram for receipts, and your choice about promotional emails.
  • Forms: the details you enter in our enquiry and book order forms (Google Forms).
  • On your device: the website keeps your sign-in token, unsubmitted test answers and a copy of recently loaded lists in your browser’s local storage so pages open faster. Signing out clears them. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

2. How we use it

To run your account, unlock what you buy, show your results and feedback, send receipts and password-reset codes, reply to enquiries, deliver books and, only if you opted in, send occasional emails about new tests and offers. Every promotional email has an unsubscribe link.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your information. We use these services to run the website, and they process data only for that purpose:

  • Razorpay (payments);
  • Google (account and purchase records in Google Sheets and Drive, Google Forms, Gmail for emails);
  • 2Factor.in (sending sign-in codes by SMS);
  • Telegram (receipts, only if you link it);
  • GitHub Pages (hosting the website) and Google Fonts (loading the typefaces).

We may disclose information if required by law.

4. How long we keep it

Account, purchase and test records are kept while your account is active and as long as needed for accounts and tax purposes. Sign-in sessions expire after 7 days.

5. Your choices

You can turn promotional emails on or off in the student portal or with the unsubscribe link, ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete your account. Write to us at the address below; we reply within 7 days.

6. Children

Students under 18 should use the website with the knowledge of a parent or guardian, who may contact us about the student’s account.
