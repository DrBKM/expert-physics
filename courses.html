HS, NEET & JEE physics

Class XI and XII physics for West Bengal HS, CBSE and other Boards, and for NEET and JEE Main. Each chapter is taught as a concept builder first, then drilled with more than ten years of past-paper problems.

You get

  • Live concept classes, chapter by chapter
  • Problem sessions built on NEET and JEE past papers
  • Chapter notes and the PHYSICS, CONNECTED books
  • NEET- and JEE-pattern mock tests for each book

B.Sc. Physics

Semester-wise coaching for B.Sc. Honours and general physics that follows your university syllabus, with lab-based understanding for the practical papers.

You get

  • Semester-wise classes on every core paper
  • Help with experiments, readings and error analysis for practicals
  • Chapter notes and practice sets
  • Unit-wise online tests

M.Sc., CSIR-NET & GATE

M.Sc. coursework and the national exams that follow it: CSIR-NET, GATE, JEST and IIT-JAM. Advanced topics, mathematical physics, quantum mechanics and research methodology.

You get

  • Unit-wise classes on the NET and GATE syllabus
  • Problem-solving sessions for Part B and Part C style questions
  • Previous-year CSIR-NET and GATE papers as timed online tests
  • Guidance on research careers and Ph.D. applications

Job exams

Physics and general science for government job exams: WBCS, TET (JTET, WBTET, CTET), SSC, PSC and RRB.

You get

  • Topic-wise classes on the science section
  • Short notes for quick revision
  • Exam-wise mock test sets with answers and hints
  • A free mock test after every four you finish in a category
