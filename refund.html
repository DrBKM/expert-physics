Refund and cancellation policy

When a payment can be refunded or cancelled, and how to ask for it.

Last updated: 23 November 2026

A. Digital study material and mock tests

This covers View, View + Download, Full Access and mock test purchases.

  1. Because digital material is delivered instantly and can be viewed, downloaded or attempted immediately, purchases are non-refundable and cannot be cancelled once access has been granted.
  2. You will receive a full refund if:
    • money was deducted but the payment failed; or
    • you were charged twice for the same item; or
    • the purchased material or test was not unlocked and we could not fix the problem within 48 hours of your complaint.
  3. To request a refund, email bipuliitr@gmail.com or WhatsApp +91 73650 23766 within 7 days of payment with your registered email and the Razorpay Payment ID (starts with “pay_”).
  4. Approved refunds are credited to the original payment method (UPI / card / bank account) within 5–7 working days. Failed payments are usually reversed automatically by your bank or Razorpay within the same period.

B. Online coaching classes

  1. A free 30-minute trial class is available before enrolling.
  2. Fees paid for a batch can be fully refunded if you cancel before the first class of the batch.
  3. After classes have started, fees for the current month are non-refundable; you may stop at the end of any month and future months will not be charged.
  4. If we cancel a batch, you will get a full refund of the fees for classes not delivered.

C. Printed books

  1. A book order can be cancelled for a full refund of the advance at any time before the book is dispatched.
  2. After dispatch, books cannot be returned. A copy that arrives damaged, or a wrong edition, is replaced free of charge if you report it with photos within 7 days of delivery.
