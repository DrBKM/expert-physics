About Dr. Bipul Kumar Mahato

Physicist, researcher and teacher. The person who writes the notes, books and tests on this site.

Teaching

The idea behind Expert Physics is simple: learn to reduce your physics load. A few core ideas, understood properly and connected to each other, carry most of the syllabus. Students who see those connections need to memorise far less and solve far more.

That is how the classes run, how the PHYSICS, CONNECTED books are written, and why every chapter ends with a timed test. Teaching covers Class XI and XII, NEET and JEE, B.Sc. and M.Sc. physics, CSIR-NET and GATE, and the science section of government job exams.

Research

Dr. Mahato completed his Ph.D. in physics at the S. N. Bose National Centre for Basic Sciences, Kolkata, in 2015. He then worked as a postdoctoral researcher at the University of California Santa Cruz and the National University of Singapore, and since 2022 at the National Institute of Education, Nanyang Technological University, Singapore.

His work is in spintronics, nanomagnetism, 2-D magnetic materials, magnonics and micromagnetic simulation, and he reviews manuscripts for about 15 international journals.
