Mock Test Rules

Please read these rules carefully before you start a timed mock test for HS, NEET, JEE, BS / MS / GATE or job exams.

  • Choose your category and open the test you want. Free tests start straight away; the others unlock after a one-time payment (per test, or Full Access for all).
  • Have a coupon code? Enter it in the payment box before you pay to get your discount.
  • Each test is timed. The timer starts when you click Start Test and keeps running even if you switch tabs or lose your connection.
  • Use a stable internet connection and do not refresh or close the page during the test, as your progress may be lost.
  • You can move between questions, mark them for review and change your answers until you submit.
  • The test is submitted automatically when the time is up. Answers you have already selected will be counted.
  • Marks are given as shown at the start of each test. Negative marking applies only where the test says so (for example NEET, JEE and GATE).
  • Do not use books, notes, calculators (unless allowed) or any other help if you want an honest score.
  • Your score, correct answers and solutions are shown after you submit. Once you have bought a test you can retake it to practise and improve; your best score is shown, and only the first attempt counts for ranking.
  • Reward: complete 4 different mock tests in one category and get 1 mock test of that category free.
  • Paid tests are for your personal use only. Sharing your login or copying questions is not allowed.
